# Research Topics

Last updated: 2026-04-24

Source: https://gitzh-chen.github.io

## Core Geometry

- Differential geometry for machine learning
  - Search terms: differential geometry; differential-geometric; tangent space; exponential map; logarithm map; geodesic; curvature; connection
  - Scope: include ML papers whose method or theory explicitly uses differential-geometric structure. Do not match generic differentiable methods.

- Riemannian geometry and manifolds
  - Search terms: Riemannian geometry; Riemannian manifold; manifold-valued; manifold neural network; Riemannian neural network; Riemannian deep learning; Riemannian metric; geodesic distance
  - Scope: core topic from the homepage research overview, especially deep learning over Riemannian manifolds.

- Metric geometry
  - Search terms: metric geometry; geodesic metric space; non-Euclidean metric; CAT(0); Alexandrov; metric measure space
  - Scope: include mathematically geometric metric-space work. Avoid generic metric learning unless geometry is explicit.

## Transport, Probability, and Metrics

- Wasserstein geometry and optimal transport
  - Search terms: Wasserstein geometry; Bures-Wasserstein; optimal transport; OT; Gromov-Wasserstein; Sinkhorn; Kantorovich; transport map; Wasserstein distance
  - Scope: include geometry-aware Wasserstein and OT methods, especially for SPD, clustering, representation learning, or neural networks.

- Information geometry
  - Search terms: information geometry; Fisher-Rao; natural gradient; statistical manifold; alpha-geometry; KL geometry; probability simplex geometry
  - Scope: include papers using information-geometric structure, not every probabilistic model.

## Non-Euclidean Spaces

- Hyperbolic and Lorentz geometry
  - Search terms: hyperbolic geometry; hyperbolic neural network; Lorentz geometry; Lorentz model; Poincare ball; gyrovector space; gyrogroup; Busemann; horosphere; hyperbolic embedding
  - Scope: homepage publications include Hyperbolic Busemann Neural Networks, Intrinsic Lorentz Neural Network, GyroBN, and hyperbolic EEG embeddings.

- SPD and matrix manifold geometry
  - Search terms: SPD manifold; symmetric positive definite; positive definite matrix; covariance matrix; matrix manifold; Log-Euclidean; affine-invariant metric; Log-Cholesky; Cholesky manifold; SPD neural network
  - Scope: include covariance/SPD/matrix-manifold geometry papers, including classification, normalization, pooling, and signal applications.

- Product, pullback, and Cholesky geometries
  - Search terms: product geometry; pullback metric; adaptive Riemannian metric; Cholesky product geometry; deformed geometry; flat Riemannian metric
  - Scope: homepage publications emphasize product geometries on Cholesky manifolds and adaptive Log-Euclidean metrics.

- Lie group and transformation geometry
  - Search terms: Lie group; Lie algebra; rotation manifold; SO(3); SE(3); group manifold; Lie group neural network; Lie group batch normalization
  - Scope: include geometric deep learning over transformation groups and rotation matrices.

- Grassmannian and subspace geometry
  - Search terms: Grassmannian; Grassmann manifold; Stiefel manifold; subspace geometry; projection manifold; matrix subspace
  - Scope: include Grassmannian, self-attention, and subspace representation methods.

## Learning Methods

- Geometric deep learning over non-Euclidean spaces
  - Search terms: geometric deep learning; non-Euclidean deep learning; non-Euclidean neural network; geometry-aware neural network; manifold learning; curved space representation
  - Scope: broad catch-all for papers clearly centered on non-Euclidean or geometry-aware learning. Require supporting abstract or title evidence.

- Riemannian optimization and normalization
  - Search terms: Riemannian optimization; manifold optimization; Riemannian batch normalization; gyro batch normalization; normalization on manifolds; Riemannian statistics; Frechet mean
  - Scope: include optimization, statistics, and normalization methods on manifolds or non-Euclidean spaces.

- Covariance and high-order pooling
  - Search terms: covariance pooling; global covariance pooling; high-order pooling; matrix function normalization; second-order pooling; covariance descriptor
  - Scope: include papers connecting covariance or high-order pooling to Riemannian or matrix-manifold geometry.

## Applications

- EEG and brain signal geometry
  - Search terms: EEG; electroencephalography; BCI; brain-computer interface; brain signal; brain foundation model; neural time series; Riemannian EEG; covariance EEG; hyperbolic EEG embedding
  - Scope: include any EEG paper, even if it does not use a geometry-based method. For broader brain-signal papers that are not explicitly EEG, prefer geometry-aware, Riemannian, covariance, or hyperbolic methods.

- Applications involving geometry
  - Search terms: geometry for applications; geometric method; geometry-aware application; Riemannian application; manifold application; hyperbolic application; Wasserstein application; optimal transport application; geometric representation for application
  - Scope: include papers where any geometry, manifold, metric, Wasserstein/OT, hyperbolic, SPD, Grassmannian, Lie group, or information-geometric method is applied to a concrete domain or task.
